Privacy Policy

Traffic analytics

We use Plausible via data.1back.link to understand page visits and installation interactions. The service receives the relevant page and ordinary technical request information. Our installation events describe the interaction, such as whether installation help was opened or a link copied. We do not add game answers, scores or the text of your messages to those events.

The external game

Starting the climb connects your browser to Crowdict. The external game manages its own requests, gameplay information, settings and any competition features. Opening the game in another tab makes the same connection.

Storage on your device

The site remembers the saved-page setting in local storage. When available, a service worker caches the site guide and its own static files for later visits. The cache excludes the external game and analytics requests. You can clear the stored preferences and cache through your browser settings.

Email correspondence

If you contact us by email, we use the details you send to read and respond to your request. For privacy questions, write to mooyuking@gmail.com.

Install Kabloom

Keep the guide one tap away. The live game needs internet access.

On iPhone or iPad, open Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On other browsers, look for Install or Add to Home Screen in the browser menu. You can also bookmark this page.
