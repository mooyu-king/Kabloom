About Us

What we make

Kabloom.cc explains the decisions behind one-use trivia rounds. We write about picking answers under time pressure, saving options for later questions and reviewing results. The examples use sample subjects and scores so visitors can follow the reasoning before starting a live round.

What you play

The game on our homepage is the Sky Climb stage of Crowdict Draft, provided through an external player. Kabloom.cc is an independent guide. The launch artwork and the illustrations of the game controls and scoring belong to the presentation of this guide.

Install Kabloom

Keep the guide one tap away. The live game needs internet access.

On iPhone or iPad, open Safari’s Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On other browsers, look for Install or Add to Home Screen in the browser menu. You can also bookmark this page.
